About us

Sara Linares, LLC is a marketing agency that looks after the social media of different businesses and entrepreneurs.

We start with the strategy: who you want to reach, what you offer, and how your content turns attention into customers. Then we run your accounts so you can focus on running your business.

Every client is billed monthly, so the work keeps moving and the plan keeps improving.
